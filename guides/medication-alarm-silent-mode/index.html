Medication alarm on silent mode: what to check

A medication reminder that plays as an alarm, not as a notification, usually rings even when the phone is on silent or vibrate. The word “usually” matters: Do Not Disturb, the alarm volume and some phone makers can still get in the way.

Notification sound and alarm sound are different

Android keeps separate volumes for calls, notifications and alarms. Silent and vibrate modes mute calls and notifications; they normally leave alarms alone, the same way an alarm clock still wakes you on a silent phone. So the first question about any reminder app is which of the two it uses.

Sonadose plays dose reminders on the alarm channel and shows them full screen. On our Samsung Galaxy S23, set to vibrate, a dose reminder played its sound at the planned time in our last release check.

What can still silence it

The alarm volume has its own slider; if it is at zero, the alarm is silent too. Do Not Disturb can be set to block alarms, so check that alarms are allowed there. The full-screen part needs its own permission on recent Android versions. And battery saving can stop a reminder from firing at all, which no sound setting fixes.

A two-minute check

  1. Turn the alarm volume up.
  2. In Do Not Disturb, allow alarms.
  3. Put the phone on silent, lock it, and run a test reminder. In Sonadose: Settings, Reliability check.
  4. If the screen shows the reminder but you hear nothing, go back to the first two steps; if nothing shows, check the battery settings.
