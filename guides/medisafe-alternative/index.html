A free Medisafe alternative after the 2026 paywall

Since January 1, 2026, Medisafe needs a subscription if you live outside the United States. If you relied on the free version, other apps cover the essentials without charging. Here is what to look for, and how to move your medications over in an afternoon.

What changed

Medisafe explains it in its own help center: outside the US, the app is now fully paid. If you already pay and it works for you, stay. Switching medication apps has a cost, even a small one.

Plenty of people are looking. On November 2, 2026, typing “medisafe a” into the Google Play search box in the UK, Canada, Australia or India suggested “medisafe app free”.

What actually matters in a free app

Feature lists look alike. What separates apps is what happens at dose time. A small notification gets swiped away without a thought; a reminder that rings like an alarm and repeats does not. Then the practical part: no account for your own medications, working offline, and no ads on the dose screen.

If you help a parent, add two more: seeing their doses from your phone, and an alert when one is missed. If an older person will use it, look for a mode with large text and few buttons.

Several apps have a free tier, MyTherapy among them. Check what the free part includes before you move your data.

Sonadose, with its limits

Sonadose is our app, so read this as the opinion of the people who make it. It is free, with no ads or subscription, and needs no account for your own medications.

At dose time it rings with an alarm sound and repeats the reminder a limited number of times (five, five minutes apart, by default). Confirming the dose stops the rest. It counts the pills left and reminds you to buy the next pack. A family member can follow your profile and get an alert when a dose is missed. In Simple Mode the reminder fills the screen, with two large buttons, Taken and Later.

Now the limits, so you do not find them later. For now it is Android only. Reminders depend on permissions and on each brand's battery saving: on the Huawei we test with, if the app is not set to manual in App launch, it stops reminding after a restart. That is why the app has a guide for each maker. Alerts to family travel over the internet and can arrive late.

How to move your medications

  1. Before uninstalling Medisafe, write down each medication with its times, or take a screenshot. Sonadose does not import from other apps.
  2. Add the medications in Sonadose. Each one is a single page: name, the dose you were told, and the times.
  3. In Settings, run the reliability check and follow the battery guide for your brand.
  4. Keep both apps for a day. Once Sonadose reminds you on time, remove the old one.

This guide is not medical advice. Doses and times are the ones your doctor or pharmacist gave you.
